# Source and credit register

**Planning snapshot checked September 27, 2026.** This register identifies sources for the brief and visual reference and candidates for implementation. Listing a source does **not** mean its data has already been ingested or every historical rule verified. Each released data file must include its exact source section, publication date or period, retrieval date, transformations, and limits. No citation below grants ownership of the source data or text to this project.

| Use | Publisher and direct source | What it supports | Current status and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| How brackets work | [Internal Revenue Service, Federal income tax rates and brackets](https://www.irs.gov/filing/federal-income-tax-rates-and-brackets) | Marginal-layer explanation: a higher rate applies only to the portion in that bracket. | Explanatory source; not a substitute for a tax-year schedule. |
| 2026 ordinary tax and deduction | [IRS, Revenue Procedure 2025-32 in Internal Revenue Bulletin 2025-45](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2025-45_IRB) | 2026 filing-status rate schedules and standard deductions used in the mockup. | Source for mockup example and future versioned rules. Rules beyond the simplified example need separate verification. |
| 2026 long-term-gain thresholds | [IRS, Revenue Procedure 2025-32, section 4.03](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2025-45_IRB) | Maximum zero-rate and 15%-rate amounts by filing status. | Candidate implementation source; not yet used to compute an image result. |
| Employee Social Security and Medicare | [Social Security Administration, Contribution and Benefit Base](https://www.ssa.gov/oact/COLA/cbb.html) | 2026 $184,500 Social Security wage base; 6.2% employee Social Security and 1.45% employee Medicare rates used in the mockup. | The mockup wage amount is below the base. High-income and multiple-employer cases need further rules. |
| Additional Medicare tax | [IRS, Topic 560](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc560) | 0.9% tax and filing-status thresholds. | Required before high-income wage scenarios can be presented as complete. |
| Capital-gain categories | [IRS, Topic 409](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409) | Long-term versus short-term gains, gain/loss terminology, and exceptional rate categories. | Explainer source. Its displayed annual thresholds must be checked against the selected tax year. |
| Qualified dividends and gains | [IRS, Publication 550, Investment Income and Expenses](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550) | Qualified dividend treatment and capital-gain calculation caveats. | Publication is year-specific; use the matching year in a built calculator. |
| Net investment income tax | [IRS, Topic 559](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc559) | 3.8% NIIT and statutory thresholds. | Applicability depends on net investment income and modified AGI; not a flat surcharge on every gain. |
| Historical ordinary tax overview | [IRS, Historical Table 23](https://www.irs.gov/statistics/soi-tax-stats-historical-table-23) | Lowest/highest regular-tax rates, exemptions, and tax base for 1913–2018. | Timeline candidate; top rates cannot stand in for a modeled household tax bill. |
| Historical return rules | [IRS, Prior year forms and instructions](https://www.irs.gov/prior-year-forms-and-instructions), including [1980 Form 1040 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i1040--1980.pdf) | Contemporaneous deductions, exemptions, Schedule D, and calculations for selected historical years. | Each calculator-enabled year needs direct, page-level verification. |
| 1980 wage maximum-tax rule | [IRS, Marginal Tax Rates, 1980](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-soi/83rpwinbul.pdf) | 1980 personal-service income was subject to a maximum-tax method limiting its marginal rate to 50% despite a 70% top regular schedule. | Must appear when contrasting 1980 wage and investment scenarios. |
| Capital-gains legislative history | [Congressional Research Service, Capital Gains Taxes: An Overview of the Issues](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R47113/R47113.1.pdf), history appendix | Explains 1978 exclusion, 1981 rate changes, and removal of the exclusion under the 1986 reform. | Contextual research; contemporaneous IRS instructions remain the computation source. |
| Inflation comparison | [Bureau of Labor Statistics, CPI databases](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/data.htm) and [BLS inflation calculator methodology](https://www.bls.gov/data/inflation_calculator_inside.htm) | CPI observations and stated annual-average convention for purchasing-power comparisons. | Choose and label a specific CPI series and completed comparison period; do not invent a full-year 2026 CPI. |
| Household capital-income distribution | [Congressional Budget Office, Distribution of Household Income, 2022](https://www.cbo.gov/publication/62300) | Context on income sources across household income groups. | Observed/model-based population estimates, not the simulator's fictional household. | Asset ownership and family finances | [Federal Reserve, Survey of Consumer Finances](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/scfindex.htm) | Wealth and asset-holding context across groups. | Survey data; identify survey year, statistic, and population in any visualization. |
| Essential spending presets, if added | [BLS, Consumer Expenditure Survey 2024](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cesan.htm) | Spending by income quintile and category. | A population average is not an individual's necessary budget. Presets must be labeled, dated, and editable. |
| Financial cushion context | [Federal Reserve, Survey of Household Economics and Decisionmaking data](https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/shed_data.htm) | Emergency-savings and economic-well-being context. | Context only; cite the exact survey question and year for any number used. |

## Calculation and claim rules for implementation

1. Keep source values, user inputs, illustrative defaults, and derived outputs visibly distinct.
2. Attach direct citations to every rule value and historical assertion, and document arithmetic for every derived result.
3. Record corrections and replaced sources in version history; never silently substitute a newer tax year.
4. Verify any third-party font, icon, chart library, or other asset license before adding it, then list its creator and license here.
5. Preserve the selected source's observation period, geography, unit, and population when comparing or charting data.
