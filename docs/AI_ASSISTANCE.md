# AI assistance and model record

**Project status: WORK IN PROGRESS.** As of September 27, 2026, this repository is a planning snapshot. It contains a brief, user stories, an implementation plan, a source register, and an AI-generated interface concept. It does not yet contain a functioning tax simulator or a validated tax calculation engine.

The project was initiated and directed by Lee Coursey. The goals and key teaching points came from the project owner: explain marginal tax brackets in plain language; compare taxation of wages and investment income; show how essential costs affect money available to invest; examine roughly a century of federal tax changes, especially the years around Reagan-era reforms; and explicitly credit all data and research sources.
