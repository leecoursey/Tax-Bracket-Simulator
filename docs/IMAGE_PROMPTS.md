## Initial generation prompt

> Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE polished high-fidelity 16:9 desktop screenshot concept for an interactive educational website called “Tax Bracket Simulator,” aimed at US adults with no tax knowledge. This is a static interface mockup, not a finished app. Clean light background #F8FAFC, white cards, deep navy text, teal for retained income, amber for the current marginal slice, blue shades for tax brackets. Serious but welcoming editorial fintech style, not childish, not corporate enterprise, no people, no decorative imagery, no gradients that reduce readability. Generous whitespace and 16px-or-larger readable UI text. Layout: top header with exact title 'Tax Bracket Simulator' and navigation 'How brackets work', 'Income sources', 'History', 'Sources'. Main hero sentence: 'Entering a higher bracket does not re-tax your earlier dollars.' Below, a clear input row: 'Annual wages $80,000', 'Filing status Single', 'Tax year 2026'. Central dominant interactive chart: gross wages flow past a labeled 'Standard deduction $16,100' into three stacked horizontal visual bracket bins at 10%, 12%, and 22%. Show exactly and legibly: '10% on $12,400 = $1,240', '12% on $38,000 = $4,560', '22% on $13,500 = $2,970'. A small amber indicator shows 'Your next dollar: 22% federal income tax' and a subtle slider control for income. Adjacent compact outcome card: 'Federal income tax $8,770', 'Social Security + Medicare $6,120', 'After federal taxes $65,110', with small note 'State tax and other deductions not included'. Lower area has two concise preview cards: one labeled 'Same extra $10,000, different source' with two clearly labeled side-by-side bars 'Wages' and 'Long-term gain' (do not add unsourced numerical results); another labeled 'Money left to invest' showing after-tax income flowing through essential costs to investable surplus with user-adjustable cost slider. Small lower navigation strip 'Explore tax history: 1980 · 1982 · 1988 · 2026'. Visible 'Sources and methods' link in footer. The visual hierarchy must emphasize the bracket bins first. Use direct labels in addition to color; distinct accessible focus rings on controls; 44px touch targets implied; no hover-only information. Avoid jargon, decorative charts, false precision, illegible tiny labels, bogus logos, emojis, or invented data. Professional design presentation quality, realistic browser viewport mockup, straight-on view, crisp exact text as much as possible.
